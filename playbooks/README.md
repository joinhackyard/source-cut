# Playbooks

How Hackyard makes evidence-based business documentaries and reels, written down so you can make your own. Every claim
is sourced and credited, real documents appear on screen with a marker on the words the narrator says, and nothing
AI-generated is passed off as a real person, headline or quote.

| Playbook                                       | What it covers                                                                                                                        | Read it when                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [series-documentary.md](series-documentary.md) | The one edit every daily film (6–8 min, 16:9) and its Short (45–55 s, 9:16) uses: beats, engine calls, two-pass checks, helper tools. | You are making a long-form documentary. Start here.                |
| [production.md](production.md)                 | The machinery: tech stack, house rules, sourcing, Azure voice and word timings, music, build, render VM, loudness, gotchas.           | You are setting up the pipeline or debugging a step.               |
| [thumbnails.md](thumbnails.md)                 | Evidence-based thumbnail rules, the A/B/C variants for Test & Compare, the v2 cut-out design, and gpt-image-2.5 with its review list. | You are packaging a finished film.                                 |
| [artifact-reel.md](artifact-reel.md)           | Format C: a 30–60 s vertical reel of founder clips and real artifacts with a marker sweep, plus page capture and Wayback sourcing.    | You are making a short-form reel. This is the current reel format. |
| [editorial-reel.md](editorial-reel.md)         | Format A: the editorial style (story beats, editing grammar, typography, grade, sound) behind a found-footage reel.                   | You want the cinematic caption-driven style, or its typography.    |
| [motion-techniques.md](motion-techniques.md)   | After Effects moves in HTML/HyperFrames: transitions, easing, motion blur, speed ramps, 2.5D parallax, sound on cuts.                 | A moment needs one extra effect. Use sparingly.                    |

The palette, fonts and CTA in these files are the house style used by Hackyard. Swap in your own brand. The honesty
rules stay.
